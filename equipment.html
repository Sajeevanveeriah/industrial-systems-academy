Identify an unfamiliar product without guessing

Brighteye Momentum is documented in a Farm Frites MES implementation. For the application remembered as beginning with A, check its role and publisher. The production software labs give the site-specific evidence and hands-on exercises.

  1. In an authorised read-only view, note the exact product title, publisher, edition and build from Help/About. Do not collect licences or credentials.
  2. Compare the identifiers with the supplier’s product documentation and the approved application inventory.
  3. Map one observed input and output to its owner before deciding whether the product handles supervision, production execution, integration or another role. If evidence conflicts, keep the mapping unconfirmed.
